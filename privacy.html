Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Vethos helps you plan your time and keep distracting apps aside while you work. This policy explains what the Vethos app collects, why, and who processes it. The short version: we keep what you create in Vethos so you can find it on any device you sign in to, we collect what we need to run your subscription and the optional AI review described below, and nothing for advertising.

Your plans and history

Your tasks, goals, anchors, schedule, sleep times, session history and settings are stored on your device, and a copy is backed up to your account so you get them back when you sign in on another device. Only your account can read this backup. It is deleted when you delete your account. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. When you choose apps to set aside, iOS gives Vethos opaque tokens, not app names. Vethos cannot see which apps you picked, which apps you open, or anything you do inside them, and none of this leaves your device.

AI review of requests to end or postpone a session

In Vethos, a work session can be ended before its planned end with the Stop button, and a session that has not started yet can be pushed to a later time with the Later button. Both ask you to write what happened.

If you allow it, Vethos uses AI to review the request: it may ask up to five follow-up questions, compare your answers with your requests from the last 30 days (older ones count less), and decide whether the session can end early or be postponed. Vethos asks for your permission the first time you write a reason, before anything is sent. To change your choice later, email us. Without permission, when you are offline, or if the review is unavailable, fixed rules decide instead and nothing is sent.

What you send goes to our server and is processed by DeepSeek, on servers that may be located outside your country, including in China. Our server links the request to your account only to limit how many reviews can be requested each day; your name and email are never sent to DeepSeek. Don't write anything you wouldn't want processed this way. Your requests and the decisions are kept with your Vethos data (on your device and in your account backup) so the review can look back over 30 days; they are deleted when you delete your account.

If what you write suggests you may be in distress, nothing is sent to the AI: Vethos pauses your plan and shows how to reach immediate help (for example, call or text 988 in the US and Canada).

Sign in with Apple and Google

We always receive an email address and an account ID. With Apple, you can choose "Hide My Email": Apple then gives us a private address that forwards our messages to your real inbox. Sharing your name is optional. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

How long we keep data

  • Account data and your backed-up Vethos data: until you delete your account.
  • Subscription records: as long as required for accounting and tax purposes, and by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.
  • Requests sent to the AI review: not stored on our server once the decision is made, apart from short-lived technical logs kept by our hosting provider. The requests and decisions themselves stay with your Vethos data until you delete your account; the review only looks at the last 30 days.
  • Abuse-limit counters: about one day.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. Your account and your backed-up data are erased from our servers immediately, and removed from that device. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all its data yourself in the app, and edit your plans at any time. To get a copy of your data, or to have it corrected, email us and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict how your data is processed.

Children

Vethos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Our server keys are kept only on the server, never in the app. Access to our database is restricted to the server itself.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
