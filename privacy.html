Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Vethos helps you plan your time and keep distracting apps aside while you work. This policy explains what the Vethos app collects, why, and who processes it. The short version: your plans, tasks and schedule stay on your device. We collect what we need to run your account, your subscription and the Coach, and nothing for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, goals, anchors, schedule, sleep times, session history and settings are stored on your device only. They are not sent to us.

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. When you choose apps to set aside, iOS gives Vethos opaque tokens, not app names. Vethos cannot see which apps you picked, which apps you open, or anything you do inside them, and none of this leaves your device.

We never sell your data, show you ads, or use advertising identifiers or analytics trackers.

The Coach

The Coach is optional. When you use it, your request goes to our server, which forwards it to DeepSeek to generate a reply. Requests are sent with an anonymous installation token, not your name or email. DeepSeek processes data on servers that may be located outside your country, including in China. Don't type anything in the Coach you wouldn't want processed this way. If a message suggests you may be in distress, the Coach does not call the model and points you to human help instead.

Sign in with Apple and Google

We receive only what Apple or Google share with us: an account ID, and your email and name if you allow it. With Apple, you can choose to hide your email; we then receive a private relay address. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Subscription records: as long as required for accounting and tax purposes, and by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.
  • Coach requests: not stored by us after the reply is sent, apart from short-lived technical logs kept by our hosting provider.
  • Abuse-limit counters: about one day.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. Your account is erased from our servers immediately. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Vethos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Our server keys are kept only on the server, never in the app. Access to our database is restricted to the server itself.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
