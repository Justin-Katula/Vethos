Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Subscription

  • Cancel or change your plan: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vethos. If you are subscribed, Profile → Manage subscription opens the same screen.
  • Restore a purchase on a new iPhone: open the subscription screen and tap Restore.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Blocking

  • Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. Vethos asks for access once; if you declined, allow it again in iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.
  • Vethos never sees which apps you picked or what you do in them.

Account

  • Sign in with Apple or Google. Your plans come back on any iPhone you sign in to.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Account → Delete account. Your data is erased from our servers immediately. This does not cancel an App Store subscription.
